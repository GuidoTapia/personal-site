¡AMIGRA, we’re part of your journey!

AMIGRA is a social LegalTech project that gives migrants in Peru reliable, accessible, and didactic information. It was built as a free application to help the migrant community stay informed and supported throughout their legal process, and it took 1st place at LegalCore's LegalCoding event in 2021.
